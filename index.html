Your devices
On the new device, open Settings, tap “Use an account from another device”, and enter this code. It works once and expires in 10 minutes. Keep this device open and online until it finishes.
Linked devices share your friends, chats and streaks while they are online together. Anyone who holds a linked device can read your chats, so keep your devices locked.
Notifications
“Name only” shows who messaged you. “Preview” also shows the text. “Generic” shows just “New message”. Notifications are encrypted from your friend’s device to yours, and the mailbox only passes them along.
Typing and last seen
These work both ways: if you turn one off, you stop seeing it from your friends too. Neither is ever stored anywhere.
Disappearing chats
Messages are deleted from this device about 24 hours after you see them. Tap a message or photo to save it, and tap again to unsave. Saved items stay on this device only.
Camera & microphone
Share Skein
“Invite” sends the link plus yours, so they can add you in one tap. On iPhone they should open it in Safari, tap Share, “Add to Home Screen”, then add you from inside the app with your ID.
Offline mailbox
Lets messages reach you, and your other devices, while you are offline. They wait locked in a mailbox and are deleted once collected. Everyone chatting needs the same link.
Chat background (all chats)
Saved on this device only. Pick one per friend from the ⋯ button in a chat.
App updates
Updates come from your Skein website on GitHub and keep all your chats. Big changes to the app itself still need a new installer.

Chat background
Only for this chat, and only on this device. Without one, this chat uses the background you set for all chats in Settings.

📷 Camera & microphone

Skein uses your camera for photos and video messages, and your microphone for voice notes and video sound. Nothing leaves your device except what you choose to send. You can change this any time in Settings.

⏳ Chats disappear

Messages, photos and videos are deleted from this device about 24 hours after you see them. This applies to your existing chats too, so tap anything you want to save first.
To save something, just tap it. It changes colour slightly and stays on this device. Tap it again to unsave. Photos, videos and voice notes also have a Download button. Saved items never leave this device.
To install Skein: tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”, and open it from there so your chats and ID are kept.
